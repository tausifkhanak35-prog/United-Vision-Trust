Our Services

Discover the programs and initiatives designed to create meaningful impact in the community.

Student Scholarship & Adoption

  • Payment of school/academic fees for deserving students
  • Career guidance and progress monitoring
  • Full adoption of students for their complete education journey
  • Regular mentorship and skill development sessions
  • Support for higher education and vocational training

Current Initiative: "Education Support Program 2024-25" - Supporting 15+ students with full academic sponsorship

Healthcare Support

  • Financial aid for purchasing life-saving drugs
  • Support for individuals and families who cannot afford essential treatment
  • Medical equipment assistance for chronic patients
  • Emergency medical fund for critical cases
  • Health awareness camps and preventive care education

Impact in 2023-24

Provided life-saving medicines to 45+ patients, supported 12 emergency medical cases

Essential Crisis Support

  • Distribution of ration kits and essential supplies to families in distress
  • Emergency support during crises and unexpected hardships
  • Seasonal assistance during Ramadan and Eid
  • Winter relief programs with warm clothing and blankets
  • Support for disaster-affected communities
