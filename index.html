FoundedApril 2020, Mysuru
“When the 2020 lockdown struck, a group of dedicated social workers in Mysore united to act where it mattered most.”

A Zakat & Sadaqah group was quickly formed, delivering ration kits to daily wage earners and vulnerable families. Recognizing the potential for lasting impact, this initiative was formally established as a registered Trust in April 2020, with Mr. Mohamed Asadulla as the founding Convener.

Today, we work across two pillars: Community Welfare and Islamic Character Building — addressing both immediate material needs and long-term ethical development.
