FoundedApril 2020, Mysuru

"United Vision Welfare & Charitable Trust was born from urgency and compassion — when the 2020 lockdown struck, we acted."

A group of dedicated social workers in Mysore united to form a Zakat & Sadaqah group, delivering ration kits to daily wage earners and vulnerable families. What started as emergency relief quickly grew into a permanent institution.

Today, we work across two pillars: Community Welfare and Islamic Character Building — addressing both immediate needs and long-term ethical development.
