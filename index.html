Welcome to United Vision

United Vision Welfare & Charitable Trust, founded in 2020, is a beacon of hope and support in Mysore. We focus on Community Welfare and Islamic Character Building, addressing both immediate needs and long-term ethical development.

Our Key Initiatives

📚

Education Support

Providing scholarships and career guidance to deserving students.

❤️

Health & Aid

Distributing life-saving drugs and essential rations to those in need.

🌱

Character Building

Nurturing youth with Islamic values and Quranic understanding through Fahm-e-Quran.

🤝

Community Harmony

Fostering brotherhood, mutual cooperation, and harmony across communities.
