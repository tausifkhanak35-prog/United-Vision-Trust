FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to contact you?

WhatsApp (+91 9535831973) is the fastest — we typically respond within minutes during office hours.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Saturday: 9 AM – 6 PM IST. Messages outside hours are responded to the next working day.

Can I visit your office?

Yes! Our office is at Subhash Nagar 2nd Main Road, Mysuru. Please call or WhatsApp ahead to schedule a visit.

How can I volunteer?

WhatsApp us with “Volunteer” to discuss available opportunities that match your skills.

Can I donate in person?

Yes, you can visit our office or arrange a meeting. Please WhatsApp beforehand to schedule a convenient time.

Do you provide donation receipts?

Absolutely. We provide official receipts for all donations and 80G tax exemption certificates upon request.
