What We Do

Explore our key programs designed to empower communities, nurture faith, and create lasting impact across Mysuru.

01

Education Support

Active Program 2024-25
01

Education Support Program

We identify deserving students from financially challenged backgrounds, pay their academic fees, and provide continuous career guidance and mentorship.

  • Payment of school and academic fees for deserving students
  • Career guidance and continuous progress monitoring
  • Full adoption of students for their complete education journey
  • Regular mentorship and skill development sessions
  • Support for higher education and vocational training
Current: Education Support Program 2024-25 — Supporting 15+ students with full academic sponsorship.
02

Healthcare Assistance

45+ Patients Supported
02

Healthcare Assistance

We provide critical financial support for purchasing life-saving medications for individuals and families who cannot afford essential treatment.

  • Financial aid for life-saving drugs and medical equipment
  • Support for individuals and families in critical need
  • Emergency medical fund for urgent cases
  • Health awareness camps and preventive care education
  • 12 emergency medical cases supported in 2023-24
03

Essential Aid Distribution

500+ Kits Distributed
03

Essential Aid Distribution

Following our founding principles, we continue distributing ration kits and essential supplies to families affected by emergencies and poverty.

  • Distribution of ration kits to families in distress
  • Emergency support during crises and hardships
  • Seasonal assistance during Ramadan and Eid
  • 200+ families supported to date
04

Character Building

Since September 2020
04

Character Building Program

Interactive online classes for children and youth in Urdu and English, including projects, tests, and quizzes covering Islamic principles and good character.

  • Covers Introduction to Islam, Pillars of Islam, Wisdom of Surahs
  • Topics: Time Management, Taharah, Good Manners, Dimensions of Imaan
  • Over 60 students graduated to advanced studies
05

Fahm-e-Quran

Since December 2021
05

Fahm-e-Quran Program

Advanced Quranic studies for members with foundational knowledge. Small groups conduct detailed Surah studies, deliver presentations, and receive scholarly feedback.

  • Small groups of 20–24 members for focused learning
  • Detailed Surah analysis and student presentations
  • Direct scholar feedback and guidance
  • Coordinator: Dr. Mohammed Haziq Nadwi

Our Approach

Program Methodology

Personalized Learning

Small group sessions ensure focused attention and direct mentor interaction for every participant.

Graduation Pathway

Students advance progressively from basic to advanced studies with structured milestones.

Continuous Assessment

Regular tests, projects, and feedback sessions ensure quality outcomes and track individual progress.
