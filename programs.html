Our Programs

Community Welfare & Charitable Programs

Education Support Program

We identify deserving students from financially challenged backgrounds, pay their academic fees, and provide continuous career guidance and motivation.

Current Focus: "Education Support Program 2024-25"

Healthcare Assistance

We provide critical support by funding life-saving drugs for individuals and families who cannot afford them, ensuring access to essential medical care.

Essential Aid Distribution

Following our founding principles, we continue to distribute ration kits and essential aid to families affected by emergencies and poverty.

Fahm-e-Quran: Islamic Studies & Character Building

Character Building Program (Since Sept 2020)

Interactive online classes in Urdu and English for children and youth, including projects, tests, and quizzes. Covers topics like Introduction to Islam, Pillars of Islam, Wisdom of Surahs, Time Management, Taharah, Good Manners, Dimensions of Imaan, and Importance of Namaz. Over 60 students have graduated to advanced studies.

Fahm-e-Quran Program (Since Dec 2021)

Advanced Quranic studies for members with basic knowledge. Small groups (20-24 members) conduct detailed studies of Surahs, deliver presentations, and receive scholarly feedback to ensure quality learning.

Program Impact & Methodology

Personalized Learning

Small group sessions for focused attention

Graduation Pathway

Students progress from basic to advanced studies

Continuous Assessment

Regular tests, projects, and feedback sessions
