Our Story

From an emergency response during the 2020 lockdown to a thriving charitable institution.

“Born from urgency and compassion — when the lockdown of 2020 struck, a group of dedicated social workers in Mysore united to act where it mattered most.”

— The Founding of United Vision Trust

Our Genesis

A Crisis That Sparked
a Movement

During the sudden lockdown in March 2020, daily wage earners and vulnerable families across Mysore faced acute food insecurity. A group of renowned social workers responded by forming a Zakat & Sadaqah group to distribute ration kits.

Recognizing the potential for lasting impact, this initiative was formally established as a registered Trust in April 2020, with Mr. Mohamed Asadulla as the founding Convener.

What began as emergency relief quickly expanded into structured programs spanning education, healthcare, spiritual development, and community harmony.

Our Purpose

Aims & Objectives

Every initiative flows from these six foundational commitments — a blueprint for meaningful, lasting community impact.

01

Community Welfare

Work for the welfare of common people through Health & Education initiatives.

02

Brotherhood & Harmony

Create brotherhood, mutual cooperation, harmony, love and affection amongst all communities.

03

Medical Support

Provide life-saving drugs to the needy who cannot afford essential treatment.

04

Education Access

Arrange continuation of education by paying fees, providing career guidance and monitoring progress.

05

Student Adoption

Adopt students from an early stage and support them until they settle into a professional career.

06

Character Development

Educate and encourage people to adhere to basic principles and develop good character.
