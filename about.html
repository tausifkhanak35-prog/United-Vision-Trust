About Us

Our Story: From a Helping Hand to a Vision for the Future

Our Genesis

The United Vision Welfare & Charitable Trust was born from urgency and compassion. During the sudden lockdown in March 2020, a group of renowned social workers in Mysore united to form a Zakat & Sadaqah group, aiming to provide ration kits to daily wage earners and families in need. Recognizing the potential for lasting impact, this initiative was formally established as a Trust in April 2020, with Mr. Mohamed Asadulla as the Convener.

Our Aims & Objectives

  • Work for the welfare of common people through Health & Education.
  • Create a sense of brotherhood, mutual cooperation, harmony, love, and affection amongst all communities.
  • Provide life-saving drugs to the needy.
  • Arrange for the continuation of education for deserving students by shouldering their fees, providing career guidance, and monitoring their progress.
  • Adopt students from an early stage and support them until they settle into a professional career.
  • Educate and encourage people to adhere to basic principles and develop good character through dedicated educational programs.
